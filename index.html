New Game

The new-game helper walks you through picking a mission and survivors, laying out the table, and dealing starting gear. It resets the round to 1, clears XP, wounds and inventories, and reshuffles the spawn deck.

🎁 Deal starting equipment
  • Make a small pile of 1 Fire Axe + 1 Crowbar + 1 Pistol, plus Pans until there's one card per survivor (e.g. 4 survivors = Axe, Crowbar, Pistol, 1 Pan; 6 survivors = 3 Pans).
  • Shuffle and deal one card to each survivor at random.
  • Phil ("Starts with a Pistol") also takes a Pistol from the Equipment deck. Then reshuffle the deck.
  • In the rulebook's words: if there are more survivors than starting weapons, "that's just too bad... everyone else gets a pan."
  • Fewer than 3 survivors? Deal from the shuffled Axe/Crowbar/Pistol and shuffle the leftovers into the Equipment deck. (our guess: the rulebook doesn't say)
  • The Tutorial (00) is different: the Pistol, Fire Axe and Crowbar go on top of the Equipment deck instead, so survivors start with Pans only.
  • The Fire Axe and Crowbar open doors. Get one of them to the door you need first!
🃏 Shuffle the two decks
  • Equipment deck (what's left of the 62 equipment cards), face down next to the board. When it runs out, reshuffle the discards, but leave out Wounded, Pans, Molotov and the pimpmobile cards.
  • Zombie deck (42 cards), face down. The Spawn tab can stand in for it. When it runs out, reshuffle the discards.

Your First Round

  • Every round goes: Players (each survivor takes 3 actions) → Zombies (they attack, move, then new ones spawn) → End (clear noise, pass first-player token). The Turn Guide tab walks you through it.
  • There are no zombies on the board at the start (unless the mission places some). They only appear in the first Zombies' Phase, at the spawn zones.
  • Don't open doors you don't need yet. Opening a building spawns zombies in every room of it.
  • Stick together. You can only trade gear with someone in your zone, and a friend with a melee weapon can clear zombies off you without hitting you.
  • XP comes from kills (1 per zombie, 5 per Abomination) and objectives (usually 5). More XP = more actions and skills, but zombies spawn at the level of whoever has the most XP.

Rules Quick Reference

Survivor actions (cost 1 each)
  • Move 1 zone. Leaving a zone with zombies costs +1 action per zombie. Entering a zone with zombies ends your move action.
  • Search — buildings only, no zombies in the zone, once per turn. Draw 1 equipment card. Free inventory reorganize afterwards.
  • Open a door — needs a weapon with the door symbol in hand. Noisy weapons add a noise token. First opening of a building: draw a zombie card for every zone inside it.
  • Melee attack — your zone; you assign hits freely.
  • Ranged attack — zone in sight & range; hits follow priority: Survivors → Walkers → Fatties/Abominations → Runners (Sniper Rifle picks targets freely).
  • Reorganize / trade with one survivor in your zone.
  • Car: get in/out, change seats, or drive 1–2 zones (runs over everything: roll 1 die per mini, 4+ hits, 1 damage, ranged priority).
  • Take/activate objective, make noise (place a noise token), or do nothing.
Zones & moving
  • A zone inside a building is one room. On the street, a zone is the stretch between two pedestrian crossings and the building walls (it can run across 2 or 4 tiles).
  • You move to a zone that shares an edge with yours. Never diagonally.
  • Street ↔ building only through an open door. Inside a building, rooms linked by an opening are connected.
Line of sight (who can see whom)
  • Streets: sight runs in straight lines parallel to the board edges, as many zones as you like, until a wall or the board edge. Never diagonally.
  • Inside a building: you see the rooms that share an opening with yours, and only 1 zone away.
  • Street into a building: any number of street zones in a straight line, but only 1 zone into the building.
  • Closed doors block sight. Open doors don't.
  • Survivors and zombies in between don't block sight.
  • Zombies use the same rules: if they can see you, they come for you.

Range & who gets hit
  • Range on a weapon card is min–max zones. 0 = your own zone (melee). A Rifle at 1–3 can't shoot into its own zone; a Sub-MG at 0–1 can shoot its own zone or the next one.
  • To shoot, the target zone must be in line of sight and in range. Ignore anyone in the zones in between.
  • Ranged hits go in this order, and the shooter can't choose: 1. other Survivors → 2. Walkers → 3. Fatties / Abomination → 4. Runners. Yes, firing into a zone with a friend hits the friend first.
  • Melee attacks and the Sniper Rifle (Rifle + Scope) choose their targets.
  • Molotov takes a Ranged action, needs no roll, and kills everything in the zone, survivors and the Abomination included. Discard it after.
Weapon damage & XP
  • 1 damage kills Walkers & Runners (1 XP each).
  • 2 damage needed for Fatties (1 XP). 1-damage hits bounce off entirely.
  • 3 damage or a Molotov for the Abomination (5 XP).
  • Each hit kills only one target — excess damage is wasted.
  • Dual weapons: two identical in hand = both fire for one action, same target zone.
  • Combos: Rifle + Scope = Sniper Rifle. Glass bottle + Gasoline = Molotov (kills everything in the zone, then discard).
Danger levels
  • Blue 0–6 XP — 3 actions, starting skill.
  • Yellow 7–18 XP — +1 action (4 total).
  • Orange 19–42 XP — choose 1 of 2 skills.
  • Red 43+ XP — choose 1 of 3 skills.
  • Zombies spawn at the level of the highest living survivor.
Zombie movement rules
  • Zombies that can see a survivor move toward them; if several groups are visible, toward the noisiest (each survivor counts as 1 noise token).
  • If none visible: toward the zone with the most noise tokens (through doors as if open if no path exists — locked doors still stop them).
  • Always the shortest route. Equal routes/targets: the group splits evenly — add zombies to keep the groups identical. Abominations never split (players choose). Added Fatties get no walker escort.
  • Out of miniatures? All zombies of that type get an extra activation instead.

Add Survivor

Solo: run 4 survivors. 2 players: 3 each. 3 players: 2 each. 4–6 players: 1 each. Remove anyone not playing with the ✕ on their panel. Panels are tinted in each survivor's figurine colour; the border shows their danger level.
Tap the round dots to spend actions. A survivor dies at 2 wounds, and each wound uses up an inventory slot. Only items in the two Hand slots can be used; moving things between hand and reserve costs an action unless you just Searched.

Each hit kills one target; extra damage is wasted. A hit with too little damage does nothing: six 1-damage hits on a Fatty just bounce off.

Ranged target order

  1. Survivors in the target zone (not the shooter)
  2. Walkers
  3. Fatties & Abominations
  4. Runners
Hits go down the list: every target in a group must die before the next group takes a hit. So a Fatty hit by a 1-damage gun soaks up the rest and shields the Runners behind it. You can shoot through occupied zones without hitting anyone in them.
Exceptions: in melee you assign hits however you like. A Sniper Rifle also picks its targets freely. A Molotov kills everything in the zone, Survivors included.

Zombie Spawn Deck

Draw one card per spawn zone, always starting at the same zone and going clockwise. Read the line matching the danger level of the highest-XP living survivor. Also draw one card per zone when a building is first opened.
Do this in step 2 of the Zombies' Phase. Spawn zones are the zones with spawn tokens on the mission map; draw one card for each, then place the zombies shown on the highlighted line.
Reminders: each Fatty spawns with 2 Walker escorts. The Abomination spawns alone; if a second one is drawn, place a Fatty + 2 Walkers instead. Extra Activation cards have no effect at Blue level. Manhole cards spawn at every manhole on tiles where survivors stand. Deck simulates the official 42-card deck; use your physical deck if you prefer.

Link Other Devices

Each phone runs its own independent companion — every player tracks their own survivors and dice. To share it: host this folder anywhere (GitHub Pages, Netlify Drop, or any web server), open the URL on one phone, then let everyone scan the QR code. Once it has loaded over the web it works fully offline, and you can use your browser's Add to Home Screen to install it like an app. Or you can simply send the file to each player (AirDrop, messenger) and they open it in their browser.
